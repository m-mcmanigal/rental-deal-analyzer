How these estimates are calculated

Rent is multiplied by unit count and annualized. Vacancy reduces rent; management and maintenance are percentages of collected rent. Taxes, insurance, HOA, and utilities are monthly fixed estimates. Capital reserve is shown in the operating expense and cash flow estimates, but excluded from NOI for cap rate and DSCR. Mortgage payment uses a standard fixed-rate amortization estimate for principal and interest only. Cash invested is down payment plus entered closing costs and improvements. Taxes, insurance, fees, financing costs, appreciation, sale costs, and tax impacts can vary; confirm assumptions before making an investment decision.
